Privacy Policy for StimTiles

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StimTiles ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use it.

Summary

StimTiles does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data directly. It has no accounts, no login, and no camera or photo library access. The one exception is advertising: StimTiles shows ads served by Google AdMob, which can collect device identifiers and usage data to serve those ads (see "Advertising" below). Everything else the app remembers stays on your own device.

Information Stored on Your Device

StimTiles saves a few things locally on your device (via standard iOS storage) so your preferences and progress persist between sessions:

  • App settings — appearance (light/dark/system), stretch tiles, haptic feedback, outlines, animations
  • Column Match win counts, tracked separately per starting-tiles mode and column count
  • Whether you've purchased Remove Ads (see "In-App Purchases" below)

None of this ever leaves your device, is transmitted to us, or is linked to your identity in any way. There's no account system, so this data isn't tied to "you" beyond the device it's stored on — uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Advertising

StimTiles shows a banner ad on the home screen (never during gameplay) served by Google AdMob, unless you've purchased Remove Ads. AdMob can collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure these ads. Because StimTiles is intended for children, ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment, which disables personalized/behavioral advertising — ads shown are generic and not built from tracking data about you or your child. Google's own privacy policy and practices govern its handling of this data; see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

In-App Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We don't receive or store your payment information — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction. Purchasing Remove Ads also stops any AdMob ad requests going forward.

Feedback Emails

If you use the "Send Feedback" option in Settings, the app opens your device's own Mail app with a pre-filled draft addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you actively choose to send that email yourself. If you do send it, we receive whatever is included in that email — typically your message, your email address, and basic app/device version information included for troubleshooting purposes. We use this solely to read and respond to your feedback, and we don't add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone else.

Data We Do Not Collect

Beyond what's described above under "Advertising," StimTiles does not use analytics services or third-party trackers. It does not collect your location, contacts, or any browsing data. There is no user account, no login, and no way for StimTiles to track you across other apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

StimTiles is designed with young users in mind, including children. It collects no personal information directly from anyone. Ads are tagged for child-directed treatment (see "Advertising" above), meaning no personalized/behavioral advertising is shown. The only way any information ever reaches us directly is if a user (or their parent/guardian) actively chooses to send a feedback email.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes again, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and describe exactly what changed, including updating the App Privacy Nutrition Label on the App Store.
