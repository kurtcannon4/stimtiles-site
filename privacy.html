Privacy Policy for StimTiles

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StimTiles ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use it.

Summary

StimTiles does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. It does not request access to your camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, or location. Everything the app remembers stays on your own device.

Information Stored on Your Device

StimTiles saves a few things locally on your device (via standard iOS storage) so your preferences and progress persist between sessions:

  • App settings — appearance, tile and animation options, haptics, sound, and accessibility preferences
  • Your game and mode preferences, list ordering, and favorites
  • Win counts and achievement progress
  • Kids Mode settings and the parental PIN, if you set one
  • Which cosmetic items you've unlocked or purchased

None of this ever leaves your device, is transmitted to us, or is linked to your identity in any way. There's no account system, so this data isn't tied to "you" beyond the device it's stored on — uninstalling the app removes it completely.

No Advertising and No Tracking

StimTiles shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. It does not use analytics services or third-party trackers, does not collect an advertising identifier (IDFA), and has no ability to track you or your child across other apps or websites.

In-App Purchases

StimTiles offers optional cosmetic in-app purchases (such as tile shapes, color sets, backgrounds, app icons, and sound packs), plus an optional tip/donation. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive or store your payment information — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction. The app only records which items you've unlocked, and only on your device.

Feedback Emails

If you use the "Send Feedback" option in Settings, the app opens your device's own Mail app with a pre-filled draft addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you actively choose to send that email yourself. If you do send it, we receive whatever is included in that email — typically your message, your email address, and basic app/device version information included for troubleshooting purposes. We use this solely to read and respond to your feedback, and we don't add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone else.

Data We Do Not Collect

StimTiles does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, browsing data, or any personal information. There is no user account and no login. The only way any information ever reaches us directly is if a user (or their parent/guardian) actively chooses to send a feedback email.

Children's Privacy

StimTiles is designed with young users in mind, including children, and includes a Kids Mode with optional parental PIN controls. The app collects no personal information from anyone, shows no advertising, and performs no tracking or profiling. The only way any information ever reaches us directly is if a parent or guardian actively chooses to send a feedback email.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes again, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and describe exactly what changed, including updating the App Privacy Nutrition Label on the App Store.
